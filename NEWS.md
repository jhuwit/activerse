# activerse 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN submission.
* `library(activerse)` attaches the core activerse packages (actibase,
  actiplot, actiread, actimetrics, actisensorlog, actisomni, actiwalkability,
  and actiquantiles), reports their versions, and lists any that are not
  installed.
* Reports function conflicts involving attached activerse packages, skipping
  the intentional re-exports of actiread SensorLog helpers by actisensorlog.
* Added the "Processing Physical Activity to a Normalized Quantile" vignette.
